## What the agent does automatically

For a qualified source contract and permitted examples, the agent can prepare data and instrumentation choices, freeze budgets, run approved comparisons, preserve identities across lost replies, and stop at an uncertain charge. Native campaigns try simple alternatives before spending reserved instruction-search effort. After a selected untouched confirmation, they can prepare the exact diff, run declared checks on independent base/proposed trees and produce a reviewable change.

The whole routing program similarly connects simple controls, conditional training/fit/validation and one confirmation under a single approved envelope. Its independent sample covers skipped training, conditional fitting, missing billing and rollback. Failed confirmation must not produce a usable proposal.

The coding agent still needs current source, authorized content/vendors, meaningful criteria and an owner budget. Source adapters and execution contracts are explicit. Unsupported dynamic code and unknown economics cause abstention. Nothing here authorizes deployment.

## Engineering still required

These are Cutback implementation responsibilities, not tasks delegated to the user:

1. **Unified optimization:** measured selection and a common execution/review contract across the currently separate native/cache, routing, retrieval, compression and training engines. Complete task economics must include independently measured non-native costs.
2. **Adapter breadth:** additional dynamic/shared/framework source consumers, remote/vector retrieval, learned reranking/compression, broader gateway providers and qualified specialty tool/application execution.
3. **Evaluation breadth:** independent judge calibration, broader attack/guardrail integrations, and the explicitly missing voice, multimodal, browser/computer and cloud-result specialties.
4. **Operations and collaboration:** sustained mixed evaluator/worker/gateway capacity, intended clean-host operational modes, remaining team dashboards/comments/provisioning and their authenticated accessible lifecycles.
5. **Release convergence:** final artifact/hosted qualification for new work and a separately versioned publication containing accepted source features. Local QA artifacts do not replace the published release.

The [120-requirement baseline](market-baseline.md) is the detailed acceptance ledger; the [implementation roadmap](implementation-roadmap.md) retains platform and specialty requirements. A long feature list or synthetic test count cannot close them.

## External evidence still required

Observed first-use trials must show an unfamiliar developer completing and explaining the example. Separately authorized application trials must establish real task quality, complete cost attribution and later outcomes. The [comparison protocol](differentiation.md) must establish an advantage over the incumbent. Adoption, willingness to pay, production reliability and fundraising are not established by synthetic tests.

**No additional account setup is required to continue engineering.** Paid provider calls, production deployment, customer outreach/data use and public customer claims require their own authorization. Private application source, names, findings and deployment details stay outside public source, examples, history and release archives.
